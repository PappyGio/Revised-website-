Our story

Making Cebu easier to discover.

LapuGo Travel & Tours is a Cebu-based travel company created to make discovering the beauty of Cebu’s islands easier, more exciting, and more convenient. We specialize in island-hopping experiences that bring travelers closer to crystal-clear waters, beautiful islands, marine adventures, and unforgettable moments.

Our website serves as your starting point for your next island adventure. Here, you can explore our featured tour packages, view the complete itinerary, check the available rates, and easily see what is included in each package.

Whether you are traveling with friends, family, a partner, or a small group, LapuGo allows you to discover a package that fits your budget, schedule, and preferred activities.

Explore the package. Check the itinerary. Know the inclusions. Choose your adventure. Then, let’s go!
